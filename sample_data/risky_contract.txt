INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

Between: Apex Dynamics LLC ("Client")
And:     The Contractor ("Contractor")
Date:    2026-08-01

1. SCOPE OF WORK
Contractor shall design and deliver a complete brand identity system, website design,
and marketing collateral, and shall perform such other duties as the Client may
reasonably require from time to time.

2. REVISIONS
Client is entitled to unlimited revisions until Client is fully satisfied with the
deliverables in Client's sole discretion.

3. COMPENSATION
Total fee: USD 6,000, payable Net 60 following Client's written acceptance of all
deliverables. No deposit or advance payment shall be made. No interest shall accrue
on late payments.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All work product, including preliminary sketches, drafts, and rejected concepts, shall
be the sole and exclusive property of Client immediately upon creation, whether or not
payment has been made. Contractor waives all moral rights.

5. PORTFOLIO
Contractor shall not display, reference, or otherwise disclose any work performed under
this Agreement in any portfolio, case study, or promotional material.

6. LIABILITY AND INDEMNITY
Contractor shall indemnify and hold Client harmless from any and all claims, damages,
and losses of any kind arising from the engagement, without limitation as to amount.

7. NON-COMPETE
For a period of three (3) years following termination, Contractor shall not provide
design services of any kind to any business operating in any market in which Client
operates or may in future operate.

8. TERMINATION
Client may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason, with no notice and
with no obligation to pay for work performed but not yet accepted.

to the exclusive jurisdiction of its courts.
