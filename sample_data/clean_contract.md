# INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

**Between:** Northwind Studio Pvt Ltd ("Client")
**And:** Hemanth Reddy ("Contractor")
**Date:** 2026-08-08

---

## 1. Scope of services

The Contractor shall design and build the deliverables itemised in the Statement of
Work attached to this agreement. Work outside that itemised scope is handled as a
written change request, and may adjust the fee and the timeline.

## 2. Revisions

Two rounds of revisions are included for each deliverable. Further rounds are billed
at the Contractor's standard hourly rate, agreed in writing before the work begins.

## 3. Fee and payment

Total fee: **INR 450,000**.

| Stage | Share |
| --- | --- |
| Deposit, before work begins | 30% |
| Second milestone | 35% |
| On final delivery | 35% |

A deposit of 30% is due before work begins. Invoices are payable Net 30 from the
invoice date.

## 4. Late payment

Overdue invoices accrue interest at 2% per month. The Contractor may pause work on any
invoice more than 14 days overdue, and the timeline extends accordingly.

## 5. Intellectual property

Copyright and other transferable intellectual property rights in the deliverables
assign to the Client on receipt of final cleared payment. Until then the Client holds a
licence to review the work.

The Contractor retains ownership of pre-existing libraries and tools, and grants the
Client a perpetual, non-exclusive licence to use them as part of the deliverables. The
Contractor retains their moral rights.

## 6. Portfolio

The Contractor may display non-confidential portions of the work in a portfolio and in
case studies once the project has publicly launched.

## 7. Liability

Each party's total liability under this agreement shall not exceed the fees actually
paid to the Contractor. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

## 8. Termination and kill fee

Either party may end this agreement on 14 days' written notice. The Client pays for all
work performed to the termination date. If the Client ends the project early, a kill fee
of 50% of the remaining contract value is payable.

## 9. Non-solicitation

For six months after the project ends, neither party will directly recruit staff who
worked on it. There is no restriction on the Contractor taking other clients.

## 10. Confidentiality

Each party protects the other's confidential information and uses it only to perform
this agreement.

## 11. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of India. The parties will attempt good-faith
resolution before either seeks relief from the courts of Bengaluru, Karnataka.

---

Signed by both parties on the date above.
